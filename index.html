How it works

Danger zone

  • MINELand mines are hidden. You can only see them while you have a Shield, and the Shield also protects you if you drive over one.
  • AIRAirstrikes show a red target circle first, then a plane flies over. They usually aim at cracked weak walls and blow them up.
  • BOXSupply drops: sometimes the plane drops a power-up by parachute instead of a bomb.

Power-ups

Every time a tank blows up it drops one. Any tank, even a bot, can drive over it to grab it.
